About Our Cybersecurity Platform

Threat Detection is an AI-powered cybersecurity platform developed to identify phishing attacks, analyze suspicious activity, and provide actionable threat intelligence using modern machine learning and OSINT integrations.

AI Threat Detection

Uses machine learning and natural language processing to identify phishing emails, suspicious patterns, and malicious communication attempts.

Threat Intelligence

Integrated with VirusTotal and Shodan APIs to gather global intelligence on IPs, services, domains, and cyber risk indicators.

Security Dashboard

Interactive dashboard allowing users to scan emails, investigate threats, monitor risk levels, and review actionable recommendations.

Our Mission

Cyber threats continue to evolve rapidly, making traditional security systems insufficient against advanced phishing and social engineering attacks.

This platform was developed to combine artificial intelligence, machine learning, and real-time threat intelligence into a single security ecosystem capable of providing faster and smarter cyber defense.

The system focuses on usability, intelligence-driven analysis, and real-world applicability for modern cybersecurity environments.
